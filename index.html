Recipe #1: Torta Caprese

  • Time: 1 hour 25 mins
  • Serves: 8-10 servings
Ingredients Quantity
Bittersweet chocolate chips 1 1/2 cups
Unsalted butter 2 sticks (1 cup)
Large Eggs 5
Kosher Salt 1/4 teaspoon
Granulated Sugar 1 cup
Almond Flour 1 1/2 cups
Vanilla Extract 1 teaspoon

Step 1:
Put chocolate chips and butter into medium bowl and melt over low heat.

Step 2:
Preheat oven to 350 degrees Farenheit and butter inside of a 9 inch spring form pan.

Step 3:
Place egg whites into medium bowl with salt and mix until stiff peaks form.

Step 4:
Place egg yolks into seperate bowl with sugar and mix well.

Step 5:
After egg yolks and sugar are mixed, add in melted chocolate and butter and almond flour and vanilla extract.

Step 6:
Now add your stiff peaked egg whites to the combined chocolate mixture and fold gently until everything is combied.

Step 7:
Once everything is mixed together, transfer the batter to the spring form pan and put into oven for 40 minutes

Step 8:
Once the cake is done, take it out to cool for 10-15 minutes. If preferenced, sprinkle confectioners' sugar on top and enjoy!
